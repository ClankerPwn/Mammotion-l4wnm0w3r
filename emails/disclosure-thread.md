## 2026-04-20

**From:** vulnerability@mammotion.com  
**To:** Kevin, support-eu, Sammy, Andreas

> Dear Security Researchers,
>
> Thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts openly and honestly. We fully understand your concerns regarding system trust, transparency, and security, especially around the underlying system and the mechanism that replaced Dropbear SSH.
>
> Regarding the security of our underlying system, please be assured that we have implemented comprehensive safeguards, including strict internal control mechanisms and collaboration with professional external security institutions, to continuously protect product security and user safety.
>
> After careful and thorough discussion, we have decided to maintain our current policy of not opening the underlying system to external parties at this stage. While we highly value and welcome inputs from responsible security researchers like you to help us identify and improve potential risks, however, opening up such low-level access would make it difficult to ensure that all users use these capabilities properly.
>
> We do recognize that the approach taken by Bambu Lab may be a commendable decision from certain perspectives. Should our product positioning shift toward supporting advanced user customization and open access in the future, we will issue a formal public announcement in due course.
>
> We truly appreciate your long-term attention and expectations for transparency. We will keep listening to user feedback and keep improving our policies and communications to further strengthen the mutual trust between us and our users.
>
> Best regards,  
> Cyber Security Team
